What the membership is, and what it isn't

We would rather you know exactly what you are buying than be disappointed in month two.

Included

Every month, whether you use it or not.

  • One number to text or call for anything wrong with the house
  • A vetted, insured local contractor matched to the job
  • Your quote reviewed against what similar jobs actually cost
  • Scheduling, reminders and follow-up handled for you
  • Priority over non-members when the network is busy
  • A running record of every repair done on your house
  • Maintenance reminders based on the age of your own systems
  • Someone in your corner if the work isn't right

Not included

Said plainly, up front.

  • Labor and materials. The contractor bills you for the work at his own rates. We do not mark it up.
  • Free handyman hours. Some memberships bundle a monthly visit. Ours does not, which is why ours costs a fraction of theirs.
  • A warranty on the work. That comes from the contractor. We hold him to it.
  • Guaranteed 2 AM response. We answer 7 AM to 7 PM. After hours we do what we can.
  • Whole-house remodels. We will point you to someone good, but big renovations are not what this is for.

    Where we work

    Greater Baton Rouge, and nowhere else until we are excellent here.

      Fair questions

      Do you do the work yourselves?

      No. We are not a contractor and we do not hold a construction license. We coordinate. The work is performed by independent licensed contractors from our network, who quote you and invoice you directly.

      Then what exactly am I paying you for?

      The part you hate. Finding someone who answers. Knowing whether he is insured. Judging whether $385 is a fair price for a job you have never had done. Getting him to actually show up Thursday. Remembering that your water heater is seven years old.

      If you already have a plumber you trust, an electrician you trust, and an HVAC guy you trust, you probably do not need us.

      Who do I pay for the repair?

      The contractor, directly, at his price. We never touch the money for the work and we do not mark it up. Contractors pay us a network fee out of their own revenue for the business we send them — which is disclosed to you here, and is why our membership is cheap.

      How fast will someone come?

      Our target is a matched contractor within 15 minutes during business hours, and most jobs scheduled within the week. Genuine emergencies — water coming in, no power, no AC in August — we push to the front. We will tell you honestly when we cannot move fast enough, rather than let you wait on us.

      What if the work isn't right?

      Tell us before you pay, if you can. We go back to the contractor, and every vendor in the network signs a re-service policy as a condition of being on our list. If a contractor will not make it right, he comes off the list — which is leverage you do not have calling a stranger off the internet.

      Are your contractors licensed and insured?

      Yes, and we check rather than take their word for it. Before any vendor gets a job from us we collect a W-9, a current certificate of general liability insurance, any license the trade requires in Louisiana, and a background screening. If a certificate lapses, that vendor stops receiving work until it is renewed.

      Can I cancel?

      Any time, from your account, without calling anyone. Monthly stops at the end of the current month. Annual is fully refundable in the first 30 days.

      Do I have to use your contractor?

      No. It is your house and your money. If you want a second quote we will help you read it.
