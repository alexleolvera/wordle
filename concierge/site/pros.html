What we expect back

Our whole product is that a member does not have to worry about who shows up. That only works if the bar is real.

1

Answer quickly

Accept or pass a job offer within the window. Passing costs you nothing; silence is what hurts the member. We watch acceptance rate and median reply time, and we route the next job accordingly.

A before photo, an after photo, and your invoice total. The photos go in the homeowner's file. The total is what our 10% is calculated from, so report every job honestly — that is the one thing that ends the relationship.

What you will need to send us

We verify this before your first job, and we re-check it when it expires. Nothing routes to a vendor whose file is incomplete.

Before your first job

  • W-9 — legal entity name, EIN, and address
  • Certificate of general liability insurance with carrier, policy number and expiry
  • Workers' compensation certificate, or a signed sole-proprietor exemption
  • Trade license where Louisiana requires one — plumbing, electrical, mechanical, or a home-improvement registration
  • Background screening consent for anyone who will be inside a member's house
  • Signed network agreement covering the 10% fee, the re-service policy and the standards above

Why we are strict about it

A homeowner is letting a stranger into their house because we said it was fine. If that stranger is uninsured, we have handed our member a problem instead of solving one — and under Louisiana law an uninsured subcontractor's injury can land on the business that sent him.

So this is not paperwork theater. An expired certificate pauses your account automatically until it is renewed. We will remind you 30 days out.

We are not your general contractor and we do not supervise your work. You contract with the homeowner directly and you carry your own insurance, as an independent business.
